CascadeLines

Ski or snowboard real Oregon trails, from the bowls at Meadows to the summit cirque on Bachelor. Meadows is built line for line from its surveyed trail map. Spin, flip, grab, grind and boost your way down, and fill each punch card to open the next mountain.

On a touch screen: left thumb steers, and spins or flips once you are in the air. Right thumb jumps, boosts and grabs.

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with any resort. Mt. Hood Meadows trails, lifts and elevations come from OpenSkiMap / OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); the snow between them is interpolated. The other four mountains are sketched from their published trail maps.
